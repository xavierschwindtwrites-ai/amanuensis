## Where I disagree with Codex

**Rare true moments.** Codex rightly cites Deci, Koestner & Ryan (1999). But that meta-analysis found that expected, tangible, controlling rewards undermine intrinsic motivation. Unexpected, informational, verbal feedback did not. In some conditions it raised intrinsic motivation. My idea 4 is exactly the safe kind: unexpected, informational, and true.

I'd keep it with three rules:
- Factual.
- At most one a day.
- Never tied to a target.

**Calibrated boldness.** The author asked to be obsessed. Codex's framing ("judge by whether starting felt easier") is wise as an evaluation rule. Still, the design can be warmer and more vivid than it allows: a chapter-end ceremony, the shelf gaining weight, hearing your story. These are pleasures, not pressure.
